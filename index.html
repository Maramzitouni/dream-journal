Dream Journal

Privacy policy & support

In short: everything you write in Dream Journal stays on your iPhone. We don’t collect, store or share any of your data, and the app has no accounts, ads or tracking.

What the app stores

Your journal entries, moods, Flip It lists, Dreams & Visions, Happy List and settings (language, theme, reminder times) are saved only in the app’s storage on your device.

They are never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server and cannot see, read or recover your entries.

What we collect

  • No personal information, no account, no email.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party services receive your data.

Notifications

If you turn on Nudges, reminders are scheduled locally by your iPhone. No data leaves your device. You can turn them off in the app or in iPhone Settings › Dream Journal › Notifications.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry or item inside the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data permanently. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, deleted data cannot be recovered.

Children

Dream Journal does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Support

Notifications don’t arrive

Check iPhone Settings › Dream Journal › Notifications and make sure they are allowed, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is off.

I changed phone

Entries are stored only on the device where you wrote them, so they don’t move to a new phone automatically.
